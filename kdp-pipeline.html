KDP FACTORY

Autonomous 3-Agent Book Writing Pipeline

01 Initial Scouting & Intelligence

Market Analysis Analyst

Before a single word is written, the system evaluates the niche and competition.

  • Analyzes Book Title & Outline for SEO potential.
  • Generates a comprehensive Market Strategy.
  • Identifies target audience pain points and keywords.

Book Architecture Architect

The Architect creates the skeleton of the book, ensuring logical flow and marketability.

  • Designs 10-chapter structures with detailed summaries.
  • Specialized "Cookbook Architect" available for recipe-heavy books.
  • Slugifies project names and initializes the workspace.
02 Content Generation Engine

The Ghostwriter Ghostwriter

Turns summaries into high-quality, 1,500 - 2,000 word chapters.

  • Adheres to unique narrative hooks and engagement patterns.
  • Injects "Pattern Interrupts" every 300 words (lists, tips, callouts).
  • Specialized "Cookbook Ghostwriter" for 80-recipe targets.

Prose Humanizer Humanizer

The final stylistic filter that removes "AI-smell" and injects human rhythm.

  • Varies sentence lengths for impact and nuance.
  • Replaces generic AI tropes with visceral, specific examples.
  • Sanitizes names (e.g., Sarah, Mark) with diverse, unique identities.
03 Quality Gates & Learning

Unbiased Reviewers Critic / Auditor

Dual-gate system to ensure the book is indistinguishable from human prose.

  • GPT-4o Quality Auditor: Checks for formatting, AI-tell phrases, and structural integrity.
  • Look Inside Critic: Cynical Amazon customer simulation focusing on Chapter 1 conversion.
  • Automated retry logic: Chapter 1 MUST pass before the pipeline continues.

The Memory Bank Long-Term Memory

Persistent feedback loop that improves every subsequent book generated.

  • Extracts "Surgical Feedback" from failed reviews and saves it globally.
  • Injects lessons learned into the Ghostwriter's prompt for all future runs.
  • Ensures the system never repeats the same stylistic or structural mistakes.
04 Compilation & Delivery

Final Manuscript Export Overseer

Automated assembly of the final product into professional publishing formats.

  • Compiles all 10 chapters into a raw manuscript.
  • Generates .docx and .epub via Pandoc.
  • Includes functional Table of Contents (TOC Depth 2) for Kindle navigation.
